## Nineteen percent

19%. That is how many patients cleared chronic Hepatitis B after a six-month course of GSK's new drug Hibsago, approved by the FDA on October 20th.

## For life

Current treatments keep the virus suppressed for life. Fewer than one in a hundred clear it in a year.

## Two firsts

Hibsago is the first antisense oligonucleotide ever approved in this disease, and the first drug ever to target a functional cure in a six-month course.

## A narrow label

The label is narrow. Adults without cirrhosis, already on antivirals for at least six months, with low surface antigen levels.

## A different frame

But 240 million people live with chronic Hep B worldwide. For an indication that was lifelong, that is a different frame.
